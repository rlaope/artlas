# Subject light and moving layers

Read for anime scenes with a passing light reveal, moving cutouts or newly revised art. Keep the approved composition and anatomy. The native-game branch retains its own palette, cells and effect treatment; these observations do not replace it.

## Separate shape from illumination

- Preserve face/neck geometry, eye outlines and anchors while allowing the lit skin and cloth planes to change luminance and colour. Freezing their RGB along with their coordinates can leave a flat pale face while only the paving reacts.
- Keep darkness selective: far cheek, underchin, wall contact and distant corners can recede while a cheek/jaw plane and a clothing/arm plane briefly emerge. Check the subject with the floor hidden; floor spill or a thin rim alone cannot establish a requested subject reveal.
- Make the existing source and occluder explain the arrival, peak and withdrawal on wall, body and ground. Project onto their different planes with coherent timing. A detached soft oval, global exposure lift or one flat screen-space stripe cannot substitute for cast light and shadow.
- Injury marks, blood and matte eyes without catchlights are conditional on the explicit request. They are not defaults for gloom. When requested, verify each named cue independently at the logical grid and encoded phone size; a vague red tint is not evidence of a particular mark.

## Revalidate masks and revealed surfaces

- After an art revision, inspect masks on that exact source. A moving garment carries its stained fabric as a connected plane; colour selection alone can split off stain islands or assign blood to a ribbon. Keep collar tips, choker and ribbon in their actual semantic layers.
- Choose backing by what the moving edge uncovers. An inner bob edge reveals underlying hair, not alley brick; an outer silhouette may reveal the room. Check both extremes and adjacent frames for gaps and doubled edges. Extract only the needed repair from a helper plate rather than replacing the approved whole artwork.
- Keep environment illumination separate from cutout-only illumination. Apply a rat's surface reveal to the translated animal, not to the fixed clean plate or its wet-ground map. Adding the source silhouette's light to both leaves a stationary luminous duplicate when the animal moves. Derive the vacated footprint's wet response from clean paving and inspect it during the brightest pass as well as in shadow.

## Judge the encoded beat

A rat's translation distance or unique frame count does not prove a scurry. At phone width, one traversal should have a legible start, travel and settle or intended edge exit. Inspect paw/body/tail timing, moving contact shadow and the vacated footprint at native scale, then confirm the beat reads without pointing out its region. Calibrate route and timing to the scene rather than prescribing a grid or amplitude. If the audio event schedule is unchanged, visual route calibration need not add sound events.

For the affected proof, keep these as separate mandatory gates when the request calls for them:

| Gate | Observable evidence in the actual encoded result |
| --- | --- |
| Subject-plane reveal | Off, peak and withdrawal differ on the intended face/neck and garment planes with the floor hidden. |
| Selective darkness | The intended focal cue remains readable while far-side/contact shadows and the receding setting stay dark. |
| Causal cast shadow | The source and existing obstruction explain the moving edge across compatible subject and environment planes. |
| Requested story cues | Each named cue survives sampling and phone presentation without changing the accepted identity or adding unrequested injury detail. |
| Living-environment contrast | The requested environmental beat reads beside the still or restrained subject; contact, revealed backing and exit stay coherent. |

Metrics support these observations. Passing format checks, attractive stills or a high average score cannot override a failed requested gate. Follow the [feedback loop](feedback-loop.md) for isolated proofs, evidence reuse and checks on the exact final master.
